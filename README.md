## Why rem?

We all save content with the best intentions. Articles to read, videos to watch, books to finish. But without accountability, they disappear into the void.

rem fixes this by:

- **Smart reminders** that nudge you at the right time
- **Simple sharing** from any app on your phone
- **AI answers** about anything you saved, right in the app
- **Clear progress** so you see your wins
- **Beautiful organization** that makes browsing enjoyable


## Features

### Save From Anywhere

Share links, images, or text directly to rem from any app. Works with your browser, social feeds, messaging apps, and more. Prefer the desktop? The Chrome extension saves the current page with one click, or Ctrl+Shift+S. Link it to your phone with an 8-character pairing code.

### Your Personal Queue

Everything you save appears in one clean, scannable list. Rich previews show titles, images, and descriptions so you know exactly what you're looking at. Sort by priority (high, medium, low), tag anything, and find it again with full-text search, online or offline.


### Smart Reminders

High, medium and low priority items remind you 3, 2 and 1 times a day, with cooldowns so rem never nags you. Set your own quiet hours, a daily notification cap, and a morning digest for what you missed.

- Snooze 30 minutes, mark as read, or lower priority, right from the notification
- Timezone-aware quiet hours (default 22:00-08:00)
- Digest at your chosen time (default 09:00) once the daily cap is reached
- Enable or disable entirely in Profile

### Ask AI About Anything

Open any saved item and tap the chat icon. Streamed answers grounded in the article's text, with optional web search when the page can't tell you. Pick from 8 free models across Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral and SambaNova, or leave it on Auto.

### Track Your Progress

Stats at a glance across 24h, 7d, 30d and 365d: minutes read, average read time, completion rate, a weekly saved-vs-read chart, and a streak that counts consecutive days with at least one finished item.

### Beautiful Organization

Articles, videos, images, books and notes are detected automatically. Add your own tags, filter by type or status (unread, in progress, read), and archive what you're done with.

### Yours, Truly

Export your entire vault to JSON at any time. Your vault works fully offline, and syncs across your devices when you're signed in. Don't want an account? rem works locally on your device too. Deleting your account deletes your vault.

## How It Works

1. **See something interesting?** Tap share and choose rem
2. **Preview appears** with title, image, and description
3. **Get reminded** based on your schedule and priorities
4. **Mark as read** when done or archive to save for later
5. **Watch your stats grow** as you build the habit


## Design Philosophy

rem is designed to feel calm, not cluttered:

- **iOS-inspired minimalism** — clean lines and thoughtful whitespace
- **Monochromatic palette** — black, white, and subtle grays
- **No emojis, no noise** — icons and typography only
- **Dark mode native** — easy on the eyes, day or night
